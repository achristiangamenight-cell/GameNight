About Us

Our mission and values as a Christian Singles community.

ABOUT US:

We're Single Christians looking to foster an environment where other Christian Singles can connect.

OBJECTIVE/MISSION:

To provide a welcoming, faith-centered and judgment-free community for single adults to mingle and build meaningful relationships. A space where we can have mature conversations and create connections.
